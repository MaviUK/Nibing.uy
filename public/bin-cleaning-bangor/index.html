Why Clean Your Wheelie Bin?

Reduce bad smells

Removing old residue helps prevent persistent waste odours, especially in warmer weather.

Improve hygiene

Regular washing removes grime from the inside, lid and common contact areas.

Discourage pests

A cleaner bin is less attractive to flies, maggots and other pests.
