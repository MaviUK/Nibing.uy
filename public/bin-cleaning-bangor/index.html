LEVEL 02 / HOW WE PLAY

How your Bangor bin clean works

1. Check your address

Enter your postcode and address when booking. The available first-clean date is based on collection information and our cleaning rounds, so check the date shown for your property.

2. Leave your bins accessible

Have the bins you booked emptied and accessible after collection. You do not need to wait at home if we can reach them. We wash bins; we do not collect or remove rubbish.

3. Wash, sanitise and deodorise

We remove residue and grime, then sanitise and deodorise your bins. Regular cleaning helps keep waste residue and lingering smells from building up between visits.

Black, blue, brown and green wheelie bins

Book the bins your household uses, including general waste, recycling and garden or food-waste bins. Choose your bin colour in the form rather than assuming every bin at your property is included.

Between visits, keep the lid closed and avoid leaving loose waste or leaking bags in your bin. Our guide to reducing bin smells and maggots has more practical tips.

How much is wheelie bin cleaning in Bangor?+

Regular domestic cleaning is £5 per bin per clean on our 4-weekly schedule. A one-off domestic clean is £15 per bin. Select your bins in the booking form to see your total before confirming.

Can I book a one-off clean instead?+

Yes. Choose the one-off option for a domestic clean at £15 per bin. Choose the 4-weekly option if you want a recurring cleaning schedule.

Which wheelie bins can I book?+

You can book black, blue, brown and green domestic wheelie bins. Choose the colour and quantity of each bin when booking, including multiple bins of the same colour.

Do you clean commercial bins in Bangor?+

Yes. We clean business bins and larger commercial containers by arrangement. Tell us the bin size, quantity and address so we can confirm the service and price.

Do I need to be at home?+

You do not need to be home if your empty bins are out and accessible. Leave the bins you booked available after collection so we can reach them.

Do you empty bins or remove rubbish?+

No. This is a bin washing service, not rubbish collection. Your bins need to be emptied before cleaning.
