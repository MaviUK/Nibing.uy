5. Booking data and Squeegee

We keep booking information and service history in Squeegee and may also securely store limited website booking status, service confirmations, digital Terms acceptance records and Customer HQ points history through our website hosting service. These separate records help us process requests, verify signed documents and manage bookings reliably.

When you submit a booking request or enquiry, your details may be sent to us and/or stored in Squeegee, our round management and scheduling platform.

We use Squeegee to manage:

  • Customer names and addresses
  • Bookings and service schedules
  • Cleaning rounds
  • Visit history
  • Customer notes
  • Customer communication
  • Service records

For customer and booking data entered into Squeegee, Ni Bin Guy remains the Data Controller. Squeegee acts as our Data Processor, meaning it stores and processes that information on our behalf so we can provide our services.

5A. Customer HQ and optional public leaderboard

Customer HQ lets eligible NI Bin Guy customers sign in by an emailed one-time code to see their own cleaning history, verified points, streak, and yearly rankings. When you request a code, we process your registered email address and sign-in details to protect access. Your private account details and history are not made available just because someone taps a public leaderboard entry.

Our public leaderboard displays a participating customer’s first name, town, XP points and ranking. It does not intentionally publish their surname, email address, street address, Squeegee customer number, or full individual cleaning and payment history. Points may reflect verified historical imports, so a displayed total is not a live account balance.

We only publish an identifiable leaderboard entry when we have recorded the customer’s affirmative permission for the specified display. We keep a record of how and when that agreement was given and whether it has been withdrawn. Previous opt-outs are respected when cleaning history is refreshed. Having an email address alone is not treated as permission.

How to withdraw: Sign in to Customer HQ, open Leaderboard Privacy and choose to remove your public entry, or email info@nibing.uy. Withdrawal removes your public leaderboard entry; it does not cancel your bin-cleaning service or erase your private cleaning points. You may also ask us to correct your displayed first name or town.

Private Customer HQ records, verified cleaning-history imports and recorded leaderboard choices are stored server-side using Netlify’s private data storage for this website. Netlify provides our hosting and storage infrastructure; Resend sends account verification emails; Squeegee is used to check an active customer’s identity. Access to private records is restricted through the website’s authenticated functions and service credentials. We do not sell leaderboard information.

6. Our lawful basis for using your information

Under UK GDPR, we must have a lawful reason for using your personal information. We rely on the following lawful bases:

Contract

We use your details to provide, arrange, or manage the bin cleaning service you have requested.

Legitimate interests

We may use your information for our legitimate business interests, such as responding to enquiries, managing our customer round, improving our services, keeping service records, preventing misuse of our website, and communicating with customers about their service.

Legal obligation

We may need to keep certain information for tax, accounting, insurance, or legal reasons.

Consent

We rely on your positive, informed consent when displaying your first name, town and cleaning points on the public leaderboard. Leaderboard participation is optional and separate from bin-cleaning services. Where required, we will also ask for your consent before marketing messages or non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We only keep personal information for as long as necessary.
